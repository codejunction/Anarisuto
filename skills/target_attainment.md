---
description: Measuring performance against targets (attainment, ahead or behind plan)
---
1. Find the fiscal quarter(s) asked about and their dates.
2. Compute net revenue per region for each quarter using the agreed definition.
3. Read the matching rows from `targets` (`region`, `fiscal_quarter`).
4. Attainment = net revenue / target × 100, rounded to one decimal place.
5. Present a row per region: net revenue, target, attainment, and the gap in money.
   Regions with no target get "no target", not zero.
