# Fiscal calendar

The fiscal year starts on **1 April** and is named after the calendar year in which it ends.

| fiscal year | runs |
|---|---|
| FY2026 | 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 |
| FY2027 | 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 |

| quarter | months |
|---|---|
| Q1 | April, May, June |
| Q2 | July, August, September |
| Q3 | October, November, December |
| Q4 | January, February, March |

So `FY2027-Q2` is 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026.

"Last quarter" means the most recent **complete** fiscal quarter. "This year" means the
current fiscal year to date. When someone gives calendar months or a calendar year, use
those dates exactly and say that you did.
